Start a chapter

Become a chapter founder.

Bring art to young people in your area and lead a meaningful initiative, with guidance and resources from Paint It Forward.

  1. Plan your first eventChoose a date and a place, such as a community centre, an outdoor space or a school room.
  2. Gather materialsCollect donations, buy supplies, or ask us about shipped supplies if cost is a barrier.
  3. Recruit volunteersInvite friends and spread the word on social media or with posters.
  4. Host the eventMake art together, take photos and count attendance so you can see your chapter grow.
  5. Keep goingHost more events, grow your volunteer team, build your social media and run fundraisers.

Who can apply

  • You are 13 or older
  • You live outside New York
  • You're interested in art

What founders do

  • Host relaxed art-making sessions with local volunteers
  • Sell art online or locally to support special education nearby
  • Run your chapter's own Instagram and TikTok

Attend

Paint with us.

Our volunteer painting sessions are relaxed, hands-on and open to young people aged 13 and up. In New York, sessions have been held in Riverside Park and in an indoor art studio. The paintings you make are sold to support local special education schools.

Questions

Good to know.

How old do I need to be?

Volunteers, leaders and chapter founders generally need to be 13 or older.

Where does the money go?

Proceeds from artwork sales fund school and art supplies for students with special needs.

I live in New York City. Can I start a chapter?

Chapters are for communities outside New York. In the New York City area you can apply to join the leadership team instead.

Where can I buy a painting?

All available paintings are in our online artwork shop, where checkout is handled.
